Camping on La Maddalena: Tenting in the Archipelago

Instead of just a day trip, the La Maddalena archipelago can also serve as a base for several days – with campsites right on the main island.

Location within the national park

Most of the archipelago is a national park, with turquoise water and coves reachable only by boat. On the main island of La Maddalena itself, several campsites serve as a starting point for excursions into the park.

Getting there

La Maddalena is reachable from Palau by ferry, with the crossing taking only about 15 to 20 minutes. This makes the archipelago easy to combine with a stay on the Costa Smeralda.

Practical tip: from La Maddalena, day trips to the neighbouring islands of Caprera, Spargi, and Budelli are possible – though the famous pink Spiaggia Rosa on Budelli is strictly off-limits to protect the sand.
If you camp in the archipelago, you don't need to plan a long ferry ride every day to reach the most beautiful coves.

Who it suits

Well suited for anyone spending several days in the La Maddalena archipelago without wanting to rely on a hotel.
