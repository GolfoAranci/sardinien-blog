The 10 Best Restaurants in Golfo Aranci

For most people, Golfo Aranci is just a ferry port and a stop on the way to La Maddalena or the Costa Smeralda. Yet the food here is genuinely good – if you know where to look. This list is deliberately subjective: not a Tripadvisor copy, but the places where you keep ending up yourself.

1. Da Morlè e Tetta

Pizza by the slice, panini and a bit of takeaway gastronomia – that is all it takes for first place. Not a Michelin candidate, but the kind of place where you end up lingering after a long beach day or on the way to the ferry. Exactly this sort of spot is missing from every generic "best restaurants" ranking, yet for us it belongs right at the top.

2. Da Andrea

A ristorante pizzeria in Baia Caddinas with an open piazza where you can sit all year round. The menu covers both worlds: fresh fish and seafood on one side, classic oven-baked pizza on the other – at fair prices and without any fuss.

3. Intro Food'n Soul – Gastrodeli

On the corner of SP82 and Via Libertà, run by Laura and Enrico: poke bowls, seared tuna and homemade seadas for dessert. More gastrodeli than classic restaurant – ideal for an easy lunch or an aperitivo, with honest prices and a kitchen that is clearly made with love.

4. Gentedimare

Right on the water, with a view of the sunset over the bay. The fried calamaretti, fregola with bottarga and the catch of the day are all recommended – a place where the location is half the battle, but the kitchen easily keeps up.

The difference between a generic "top 10 restaurants" article and a real insider list usually shows at number 1 – not at number 10.

5. Blu Restaurant (Hotel Gabbiano Azzurro)

Only a few metres above the sea and recognised by the L'Espresso guide. Head chef Daniele Sechi is known for his fregula nera – a Sardinian pasta variety made with cuttlefish ink. Open only from May to October, but with a view over the whole bay of Golfo Aranci.

6. La Spigola

Listed in the Michelin Guide since 2025 ("Good cooking"), with chef and owner Roberto Pisano cooking with short supply chains – vegetables from the restaurant's own garden, cheese from its own sheep, plus fish from the region. Right on the beach with its own beach club, named "Best Beach Restaurant Sardegna" in 2024. The cooking is undoubtedly excellent – but anyone who cares about down-to-earth prices should factor that in beforehand, because it is not cheap here.

7. Sottomonte da Giovannino

Family-run since 1978, on the panoramic road towards Olbia. The strength is the grill: lamb, kid, steaks and ribs, plus fresh fish such as sea bream or spiny lobster. Two dining rooms, one of them outdoors, with a view of a small park with a pool – also well suited to larger groups or celebrations.

8. Giampippo

Solid, unfussy cooking with a good reputation for seafood starters and a tuna fillet that is regularly praised. Not a place you visit for the architecture, but for what ends up on the plate.

9. Chiaro di Luna

A pizzeria and ristorante on Via Libertà, on a terrace just a few steps from the sea. The pizza comes from a wood-fired oven, alongside Sardinian fish and meat dishes with local cheese and cured meats – a reliable choice when your first choice is fully booked.

10. Trattoria del Porto

Right on the marina since 2014, with a view of the moored boats. The focus is clearly on fish: from raw antipasti and fried dishes to pasta with clams and bottarga. A dependable nightcap to close this list – and a lovely place to end your last evening before the ferry crossing.

Practical tip: In high season (July/August) it is worth booking a table the day before at every place on this list – Golfo Aranci is small, and the good seats go quickly. Some restaurants (for example Blu Restaurant) also have seasonal opening hours only.
