Albero Capovolto: Small Design Hotel Near Olbia

Only seven rooms, but with plenty of attention to detail: Albero Capovolto near Olbia bets on small scale rather than a large resort.

Concept

With just seven rooms, the property is among the smaller boutique hotels on the island. The focus is on thoughtful interior design and a kitchen that consistently draws on local Sardinian produce.

Location

The hotel is about 15 minutes' drive from Olbia's airport and city – practical as the first or last stop of a Sardinia trip, without sitting in the middle of the tourist centre.

Good to know: because of the small number of rooms, the property books up quickly – anyone interested should reserve early, especially in peak season.
Not every remarkable hotel in Sardinia needs its own private beach.

Who it suits

Well suited for travelers looking for a calm but stylish place to stay shortly after arriving or before departing from Olbia.
