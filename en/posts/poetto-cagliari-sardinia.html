Spiaggia del Poetto: Cagliari's City Beach

About eight kilometres of fine sandy beach right by the island's capital: the Poetto proves you don't need to stay somewhere remote for a great beach day in Sardinia.

Location & getting there

The Poetto is just a few minutes' drive (or a bus ride) from central Cagliari, making it one of the easiest good beaches on the island to reach. A promenade runs along its entire length.

The Sella del Diavolo

At the eastern end of the beach, the Sella del Diavolo ("the Devil's Saddle") juts out into the sea – a striking rocky headland with hiking trails and views over the Bay of Cagliari.

Practical tip: The Poetto is great for combining a city trip to Cagliari with a beach day, no car needed – ideal for travellers without a rental car, or as a last stop before a flight from Cagliari-Elmas.
Few Mediterranean island capitals have such a good beach right on their doorstep.

Infrastructure

Numerous bars, restaurants, and beach clubs (stabilimenti) line the Poetto – from simple to upscale, there's something for every taste. If you'd rather find a free, unmanaged stretch, you'll find that too along the long shoreline.
