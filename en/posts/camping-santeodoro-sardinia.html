Camping San Teodoro: Right on La Cinta

Few campsites on the island sit as close to one of its best-known sandy beaches as this one: right on the five-kilometre-long La Cinta near San Teodoro.

Location

The site spreads out under dense tree cover, which provides natural shade and a pleasant climate even on hot summer days – an advantage over more open sites without any greenery.

Beach right outside

Direct access to La Cinta means short distances: no car needed to reach one of Sardinia's most beautiful sandy beaches. Behind it also lies the San Teodoro lagoon with its flamingos.

Practical tip: San Teodoro itself offers good infrastructure with restaurants and shops within walking distance – handy if you don't want to drive out to shop every day.
A campsite right on one of the island's best-known beaches, with no car needed at all.

Who it suits

Well suited for anyone who values a long sandy beach with good infrastructure nearby and appreciates shade at their pitch.
