Costa Rei: A Kilometres-Long Sandy Beach in the Southeast

White, fine-grained sand stretching for several kilometres, and a sea that slopes gently and evenly: Costa Rei ranks among the most family-friendly of Sardinia's longer sandy beaches.

Location & getting there

Costa Rei lies in the southeast of the island, about a 50-minute drive from Cagliari. The village itself is well set up for tourism, with numerous resorts and accommodations close to the beach.

What makes the beach special

Unlike the narrow coves in the Golfo di Orosei, Costa Rei is an open, elongated sandy beach with plenty of room even in high season. The gently sloping water suits children and inexperienced swimmers well.

Practical tip: If you want peace and quiet, head to the edge of the built-up area – the beach gets noticeably calmer there, without any drop in water quality.
Costa Rei shows that long sandy beaches in Sardinia don't have to be crowded.

Infrastructure

Restaurants, supermarkets, and watersport rental stands are well stocked in high season; outside the season many businesses close, so check ahead if you're travelling outside July/August.
