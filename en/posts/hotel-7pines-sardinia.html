7Pines Resort Sardinia: Modern Luxury in Baja Sardinia

If you're after contemporary design rather than historic stone architecture, the 7Pines Resort near Baja Sardinia offers a modern alternative to the Costa Smeralda's more traditional properties.

Concept

The resort favors clean, modern architecture, generous rooms, and several pools with panoramic views over the Gallura coast – a clear contrast to the traditionally rustic stone buildings of other hotels in the region.

Location

Baja Sardinia sits on the Costa Smeralda, just a few minutes' drive from Porto Cervo, with its own stretch of beach and views of the offshore islands of the Maddalena group.

Good to know: as a larger modern resort, the 7Pines offers more infrastructure (several restaurants, spa, pools) than the smaller boutique properties in the region – good for anyone who values a comprehensive on-site offering.
Not every remarkable hotel on the Costa Smeralda relies on 1960s stone architecture.

Who it suits

Suits travelers who prefer modern comfort and an extensive resort offering over the classic, more rustic style of building.
