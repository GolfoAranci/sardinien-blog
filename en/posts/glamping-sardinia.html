Glamping in Sardinia: Camping Without Compromise

Anyone who loves the nature of a campsite but doesn't want to give up a comfortable bed and a private bathroom will find a growing number of glamping options in Sardinia.

What glamping is

Instead of your own tent, you stay in permanently set-up lodge tents with wooden floors, a real bed, a private bathroom, and often high-quality furnishings – campsite atmosphere with hotel comfort.

Examples on the island

Near Porto San Paolo, with a view of the island of Tavolara, operators like Welcome Farm Glamping offer luxurious tents set among lavender fields. Other resorts, for example near Torre del Porticciolo in the northwest, favor accommodations that deliberately blend into the landscape.

Good to know: glamping accommodations cost noticeably more than a regular tent pitch, but are usually still cheaper than a comparable hotel room right on the sea.
Glamping is for anyone who loves the camping atmosphere but doesn't want to give up a private bathroom.

Who it suits

A good option for camping beginners or anyone who values being close to nature but isn't willing to give up comfort.
