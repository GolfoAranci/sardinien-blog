Tuerredda: A Turquoise Bay With a Small Island in the Southwest

A small bay with pale sand, turquoise water and a rocky island a few strokes off the shore: Tuerredda near Teulada is one of the most beautiful beaches in the southwest – and in high season it can only be visited with a reservation.

Location

Tuerredda lies in the municipality of Teulada in the southwest of Sardinia, not far from Chia. About 150 metres off the shore lies the small rocky island of Isola di Tuerredda – a popular target for swimmers and snorkellers.

Beach & water

The sand is pale and fine, and the water glows turquoise and stays shallow near the shore. That makes the bay attractive for families too (more in our article Sardinia With Kids). Reddish rocks line the edge of the beach, with the island in the background.

A bay that looks like a postcard – which is exactly why it has to be regulated.

Booking in the 2026 season

From 2 June to 11 October 2026, access to the beach is only possible with an online reservation. Bookings are made at spiaggiatuerredda.it, and you show the QR code you receive at the entrance. According to reports on the 2026 season, the reservation costs 3 euros per person (children under 12 free); at most 1,100 visitors are admitted per day, one booking covers up to six people and can be made no earlier than 24 hours in advance. The exact conditions can change every year – please check the official site.

Practical tip: Outside the booking period the rules of the municipality of Teulada apply; check them before you travel. A nearby alternative is Chia.
