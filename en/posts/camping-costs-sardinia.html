Camping Costs Compared to Hotels & Vacation Rentals

Camping is considered the cheapest way to travel around Sardinia – but how big the difference to a hotel or vacation rental really is depends heavily on season and amenities.

Campsite

A pitch for a tent or small motorhome costs roughly between 20 and 50 euros a night depending on location and season, more in very sought-after coastal spots. Mobile homes or fixed bungalows at campsites are usually noticeably pricier.

Hotel & vacation rental

Simple hotels or vacation rentals away from the Costa Smeralda often start around 80 to 120 euros a night in the shoulder season, noticeably more in peak season and in sought-after locations.

Practical tip: camping pays off financially most for longer stays and for families or groups sharing a pitch – for short stays, the price advantage over a simple vacation rental shrinks noticeably.
The biggest price advantage of camping doesn't come from the overnight stay alone, but from cooking your own food instead of eating out every day.

What really makes the difference

Beyond the accommodation itself, campers often save additionally because they cook more themselves instead of eating out constantly – a factor frequently underestimated in pure overnight-price comparisons.
