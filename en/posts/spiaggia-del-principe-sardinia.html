Spiaggia del Principe: The Bay on the Costa Smeralda

Between granite rocks and macchia near Arzachena lies a crescent-shaped bay with pale sand and shallow, turquoise water. Spiaggia del Principe is one of the best-known beaches on the Costa Smeralda.

Location & access

The beach lies on the Costa Smeralda in the municipality of Arzachena, close to the bay of Cala di Volpe (home of the Hotel Cala di Volpe). From the car park, an unpaved, rocky path leads to the beach – depending on the source about ten minutes or roughly eight hundred metres. Sturdy shoes are more comfortable than flip-flops.

Sand & water

The sand is pale and fine, mostly quartz sand, framed by reddish granite rocks. The water stays shallow for a long way and is remarkably clear. The name recalls the Aga Khan, who had the Costa Smeralda developed as a holiday coast from the 1960s onwards.

Pale sand, granite and turquoise – the Costa Smeralda in a single bay.

Practical information

There are no facilities on the beach itself; in some places vendors offer umbrellas and loungers. Bring water, food and sun protection. In high season the car park fills up early – reportedly by about nine o'clock in July and August – and parking is paid in summer. Prices change from year to year, so check on site.

Practical tip: Arriving early in the morning or outside July and August makes the bay much quieter. Many Costa Smeralda beaches lie close together – an overview is in our beaches article.
