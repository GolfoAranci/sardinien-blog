Porto Giunco: Beach, Watchtower and Flamingo Lagoon

Pale sand with a pink tinge, a watchtower on the rock and a lagoon with flamingos behind it: Porto Giunco near Villasimius is one of the most varied beaches in the island's southeast.

Location

Porto Giunco lies near Villasimius in the far southeast of the island, according to Sardegna Turismo about 45 to 65 kilometres from Cagliari.

Beach & water

The beach is a long strip of soft, pale sand whose pink tinge comes from granite fragments. The sandy bottom slopes gently, and the bay lies between two headlands that shelter it from the wind – good conditions for families with children. Because a tongue of sand separates the sea from the pond behind it, it is sometimes called the “beach of the two seas”.

Sea in front, lagoon behind and a watchtower in between.

Tower & lagoon

On the southwestern headland stands the Torre di Porto Giunco, about nine metres high, built by the Spanish as protection against pirate raids. Behind the beach lies the Stagno di Notteri, home to flamingos and other migratory birds. How many birds you see varies with the season.

Protected area

Porto Giunco is part of the westernmost section of the Capo Carbonara marine protected area, which covers about 86 square kilometres. So please: do not disturb the birds, do not enter the sensitive shore of the lagoon and take nothing with you.

Practical tip: Further north lies Costa Rei with its long, gently sloping beach, and in the other direction Cagliari with the Poetto – both fit well into a day in the southeast.
