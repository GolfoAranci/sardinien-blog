Cala Sisine: The Bay at the End of the Gorge

Cala Sisine lies at the end of the Codula di Sisine, a deep gorge on the steep coast near Baunei. The beach is a good hundred metres deep and about five hundred metres long – one of the larger bays in the Gulf of Orosei.

Location & getting there

Cala Sisine lies in the Gulf of Orosei near Baunei, roughly between Cala Goloritzé and Cala Luna, at the mouth of the Codula di Sisine – a deep gorge that runs from the mountains down to the sea. The most comfortable way to get there is by boat, for example on a boat tour from Cala Gonone or Arbatax (more in our article on bookable tours).

By land, a dirt road branches off the Golgo plateau about 200 metres before the church of San Pietro. It follows the stream of the Codula for around 13 kilometres to the Planu 'e Murta car park; from there a path leads to the beach in about half an hour. According to the municipality of Baunei, drivers without a high-clearance vehicle have to park at Ololbissi and walk about eight kilometres (Baunei tourism page).

Beach & water

At a good hundred metres deep and about five hundred metres long, the bay is larger than many other beaches on the east coast. According to several guides the beach consists mostly of pale pebbles, so swimming shoes are a good idea. Behind it, the wooded walls of the gorge rise steeply – a view that works especially well from the water.

A broad bay you can reach by boat or on foot, depending on how much energy you have.

Food & drink

The tavern Su Coile lies inland. Beyond that there is little infrastructure: bring water, sun protection and food.

Practical tip: Many bays in the Gulf of Orosei have visitor limits or booking requirements. The sources we checked mention no requirement for Cala Sisine, but rules change – before your trip, check the municipality of Baunei (more addresses on our links page).
