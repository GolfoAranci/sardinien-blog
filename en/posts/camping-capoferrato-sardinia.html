Camping Village Capo Ferrato: Between Juniper and Dunes

Set right among juniper trees, dunes, and a gently sloping sandy beach, this site near Costa Rei is one of the most family-friendly campsites on the southeast coast.

Location & grounds

The site sits directly on the beach at Costa Rei, embedded in Mediterranean scrubland with juniper and dune vegetation. Access to the water is short, and the gently sloping sea suits young children well.

Facilities

Pitches are generously sized at 80 to 120 square metres. For families, there are child-friendly bathroom facilities including changing tables – a detail not every campsite on the island offers.

Practical tip: Costa Rei is in the southeast of the island, about 50 minutes' drive from Cagliari – see also our article on the beach itself.
A campsite that actively caters to families with young children, rather than just tolerating them.

Who it suits

Especially recommended for families who value a long, easy sandy beach right outside the tent flap.
