S'Isuledda: A Shallow Sandy Beach Near San Teodoro

About five hundred metres of fine white sand, a seabed that stays shallow for a long way, and dunes with old juniper trees behind it: S'Isuledda lies at Puntaldia in the municipality of San Teodoro and suits families well.

Location

S'Isuledda – also called L'Isuledda – lies at Punta dell'Isuledda near Puntaldìa in the municipality of San Teodoro. The headland marks the southern boundary of the Tavolara – Punta Coda Cavallo marine protected area. From here the view takes in the island of Tavolara, Capo Coda Cavallo and Puntaldìa (more about the island in our excursions article).

Beach & water

The beach is about 500 metres long with pale, fine sand. The seabed slopes very gently, and the water stays shallow for many metres – ideal for children. Rocks at the ends are good for snorkelling. Behind the beach grow junipers, some of them centuries old, and macchia with broom, myrtle and strawberry tree; next to it lies a lagoon that dries out in summer.

Shallow, white and unhurried – a family beach with a view of Tavolara.

Facilities

There is parking and rental of loungers, umbrellas, pedal boats, canoes and windsurfing gear. On windy days the waves bring out the surfers.

Practical tip: Several beaches lie close together in this corner of San Teodoro – for example La Cinta and Cala Brandinchi. A day works well with two stops.
