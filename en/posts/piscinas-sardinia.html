Piscinas: The Shifting Dunes of the Costa Verde

Sand hills up to 60 metres high, seven kilometres along the coast: the dunes of Piscinas on the Costa Verde are considered one of Europe's largest dune landscapes – far from rows of umbrellas.

Location & getting there

Piscinas lies on the Costa Verde in the municipality of Arbus on Sardinia's west coast. The drive leads along the SP 126 past the former Montevecchio mines. Beyond Ingurtosu, an abandoned mining settlement, the asphalt gives way to a gravel track of around ten kilometres according to guides – drive slowly and allow plenty of time.

Dunes & nature

According to Sardegna Turismo, the dunes reach up to 60 metres in height and stretch for about seven kilometres along the coast. The fine, golden sand is shaped by the mistral. Junipers and lentisk grow between the dunes, Sardinian deer are occasionally seen, and sea turtles lay their eggs on the beach.

A landscape that recalls the Sahara more than a beach holiday.

On site

The beach has two kiosk restaurants and equipment rental, with a campsite and a hotel nearby (more on camping in our article Camping on the Costa Verde). The steady wind draws surfers, windsurfers and kitesurfers.

Practical tip: Dunes are fragile – stay on the paths and take nothing with you. Sun protection and plenty of water are a must; walks through the dunes are harder than they look. A very different sandy beach lies further north: Is Arutas with its quartz sand.
